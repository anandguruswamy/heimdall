HEIMDALL

Room-wide radar mesh, from $30 radios

UWB radios were invented to answer one question — how far away is the key fob? Every pulse bounces off the whole room. We built a radar out of five of them.

01 · Why UWB

Every ranging radio is secretly a radar.

01

A UWB radio fires a short pulse. It bounces off walls, furniture, and people — and the radio records every echo.

02

Pulses are sub-nanosecond wide, so each echo is separated in time — a full reflected-energy profile of the room.

03

No cameras. Works in the dark, through smoke and non-metal walls — and it records where things are, never what you look like.

Today the industry uses it for one thing — IS THE KEY FOB IN RANGE?  →  the least interesting measurement it makes.

The engineering that matters

Radio timing never waits for the USB link. When the host falls behind we drop-newest with visible sequence gaps — the radio schedule is never perturbed.
